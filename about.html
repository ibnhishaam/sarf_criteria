How it works

What this site is

These are the Sarf criteria for this year. Each one describes something you should be able to do, so you can see what you have learned and what still needs work. Learn each one with an example, even when the line does not ask for one.

The criteria

  • I have arranged them in sections. You can also view them by topic, which gathers lines from different sections, such as every rule for a weak first root letter.
  • Use Show only at the top to choose which lines you see. Every box starts switched on; click one to hide those lines, for example the bulb lines, or the lines you have marked High.
  • The i at the end of a line tells you its ID, its year, its section, its topics and your notes on it.

The application page

  • This page applies to any word you are given, rather than to one topic.
  • Work backwards: identify each feature of the word, and each part it has. The possible answers are shown in grey after each one.
  • Work forwards: construct a word when you are given its features and parts.
  • Each row has its own confidence bar.

Your confidence bar

  • The bar at the start of each line shows how confident you are in that criterion, from empty to full.
  • Click the left, middle or right of the bar for low, medium or high. Click the same level again to step down one.

The glossary

  • The key terms, each with its definition. The i shows the transliteration, the English and the plural.
  • Each term has a confidence bar too.

Your notebook

  • A place to write your own notes. Link each note to a criterion, a topic or a section.
  • One note can be linked to several criteria, for example when you compare two rules.
  • Open a note from the i on any line, or start one in the notebook.
  • Quote puts a criterion's wording into your note.

Your progress

  • A dashboard to see how much you have covered and what you have left to do, by topic and by section.
  • Open a topic or section to see its lines and set your bars there.
  • The i beside each count shows how many lines are at each step.
  • Choose which priorities to count, for example only the high ones before an exam.

Settings

  • Open Settings from the top right of any page. The page behind shows each change as you make it.
  • Sarf terms: show them in Arabic, in transliteration or in English, whichever helps you learn best.
  • Your year: every criterion applies to Year 2. Some cover topics Year 1 has not been taught yet, and these carry a Y2 tag.
  • In Year 1, choose Year 1 to hide the criteria that do not apply to you yet.
  • In Year 2, choose Year 2 to remove the Y2 tags, since every criterion applies to you.
  • You can also show the priority as squares or as a bar, and choose the Arabic and English fonts.

Where your work is saved

  • Your bars, notes and settings are saved in the browser on the device you are using, for you alone. I cannot see them.
  • They do not move between devices, and clearing your browsing data removes them.
  • In the notebook, download a backup now and then. It is the only way to keep your work safe or move it to another device. Restore backup reads it back.

Questions and feedback

  • Each criterion has its own ID: the grey tag in its i.
  • If a criterion is unclear, let me know through the contact form and quote its ID, so I know exactly which one you mean.
  • The contact form is also there for any other question, suggestion or feedback.
