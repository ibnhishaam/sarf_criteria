How it works

What this list is

These are the Sarf criteria for this year. Each one describes something you should be able to do, so you can use the list to see what you have learned and what still needs work.

Ticking off criteria

  • When you can do a criterion without help, tick it off.
  • Your ticks are for you alone. They are saved in the browser on the device you are using, so they are still there the next time you open the page.
  • They do not move between devices, and clearing your browsing data removes them.

Settings

  • Sarf terms: show them in Arabic, in transliteration or in English, whichever helps you learn best.
  • Your year: every criterion applies to Year 2. Some cover topics Year 1 has not been taught yet, and these carry a Y2 tag.
  • In Year 1, choose Year 1 to hide the criteria that do not apply to you yet.
  • In Year 2, choose Year 2 to remove the Y2 tags, since every criterion applies to you.

Questions and feedback

  • Each criterion has its own ID: the grey tag at the end of its line.
  • If a criterion is unclear, let me know through the contact form and quote its ID, so I know exactly which one you mean.
  • The contact form is also there for any other question, suggestion or feedback.
